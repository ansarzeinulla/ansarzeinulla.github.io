Research

First strong solution of Bestemshe

C++ · Python · ZSTD

A five-pit Kazakh mancala, solved exactly for all 125,698,242,527 positions: the second player wins. No one had solved it before. For scale, the strong solution of Awari (2002) took 778 GiB on a 72-node cluster; this one is 8.96 GB and answers any position over a single HTTP range request, with no backend at all.

126 · 109positions solved exactly
8.96 GBO(1) lookup, static storage

LLM for a low-resource language, trained on a laptop

MLX · LoRA · Hugging Face

A 2.35M-word corpus for Nogai — 86,000 speakers, in no major model — built by a reproducible pipeline, then Qwen2.5-1.5B adapted to it on Apple Silicon. No cluster. I found train/validation leakage in my own data, rebuilt the splits, and withdrew a journal submission rather than publish numbers I could not defend.
